About

Cyberpunk 2077

Cyberpunk 2077 is an open-world, action-adventure RPG set in the dark future of Night City — a dangerous megalopolis obsessed with power, glamour, and body modification. You play as V, a mercenary outlaw going after a one-of-a-kind implant that is the key to immortality.

Developed by CD Projekt RED, the creators of The Witcher series, Cyberpunk 2077 pushes the boundaries of interactive storytelling with its deep narrative choices, stunning visuals, and unparalleled world-building.

  • Explore 6 unique districts of Night City
  • Over 100 hours of story-driven content
  • Deep RPG progression with cybernetic augmentations
  • Dynamic open world with day-night cycle
  • Multiple endings based on your choices
